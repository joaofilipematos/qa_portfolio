About Me

Hello! I'm João Filipe Matos. I live in the interior of Portugal in Fundão—a place I've called home for most of my life because of its slower-paced lifestyle and close connection to nature.


If you looked at my resume a few years ago, you'd see a degree in Tourism and a background in customer service. While I enjoyed the dynamic of working with the public, I craved a career path with more robust technical growth. Combined with my natural obsession with details and tech, that drive led me to shift my career into software engineering in my late 20s.

It turned out to be the right move. Today, I work as a consultant test engineer, helping international teams deliver reliable software across diverse industries like telecom and automotive. I finally found something I love doing every day, and I'm always looking for the next technical challenge to tackle.


So far, I've worked in both manual and automation testing with very diverse teams, and I can confidently say that I thrive in projects where quality and communication are front and center. My background in customer service gave me a unique perspective on client dedication, teaching me that true quality means strong communication, high efficiency, and delivering real value.

That is why I thrive with agile methodologies and love leveraging AI as a tool to boost delivery efficiency, assist with research, and streamline processes.


If you are looking for a test engineer with expertise in test automations, AI ready, detail oriented and a team player, feel free to reach out!
